## Overview

Aesthetic Trainer combines workout organization, exercise reference content, and progress records in a single web application. It is built for users who want an easier way to arrange aesthetic-focused training and keep their fitness information available for review.

Plan sessions, look up exercise guidance before or during a workout, and update your training history as you go. Keeping these activities together provides a straightforward way to follow routines consistently without relying on multiple separate tools.
